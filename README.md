I'm an AI/ML Engineering student who learns by building. My current focus sits at the intersection of backend engineering and applied AI — writing APIs in Python and FastAPI, practicing DSA to stay sharp on fundamentals, and exploring how LLMs, RAG, and AI agents move from theory into working systems.
